# SUNDIAL · La Meridiana – Print Enquiry

PVA (Photovoltaic Art) is a solar-powered art trail in Bosa: nine old solar panels around the town each record a day of sun and turn it into an image. After the exhibition, all of it becomes one four-colour screen print, and we would like your shop to print and sell it. This page outlines the print and lists what we need to agree. An A4 sample of the images is attached.

## The print

| Item | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Layout | Square, 9 × 9 grid: 63 image tiles (9 panels × 7 days); "SUNDIAL · La Meridiana" centred in the top row, the dates centred in the bottom row |
| Colours | 4 spot inks, printed light to dark: ochre, red, blue, black |
| Overprint | Inks translucent, so red over ochre or blue makes a third colour |
| Artwork | Flat vector shapes, no gradients or halftones; smallest detail about 1–1.5 mm |
| Files | We supply the four separations at print size, with registration marks, ready for film |
| Size | As large as your biggest screen allows, image square, paper square |

## What we propose

The shop makes the screens, prints, and sells the prints, which can be ordered at any time after the exhibition. PVA keeps ownership of the screens (or the films, if that is more practical). Every print should match the first, whenever it is made.

## Questions

1. **Print area:** what is the largest image you can print on your biggest screen? This sets the size of every tile.
2. **Paper:** which paper would you suggest for four colours, and can you cut it square (for example 70 × 70 cm from 70 × 100)?
3. **Inks:** do you print water-based inks on paper, and can you add transparent base for the overprint? Can you record the mix so later prints match?
4. **Registration:** can you register four colours accurately, by press, table or pins?
5. **Proof:** could you pull a small test of ochre over red on the chosen paper?
6. **Screens or films:** is it better for PVA to keep four screens, or the films and have screens re-made when needed? Where would they be stored?
7. **Edition:** open (printed to order) or limited and numbered? What would you recommend?
8. **Costs and sales:** set-up costs (films, screens), price per print, and how sale proceeds would be shared.
9. **Timing:** how long from receiving files to the first prints?
10. **Selling:** in the shop, online, or both?
